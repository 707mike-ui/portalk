Lawrence Beckner (b. Bet. 1733 - 1802)

Lawrence Beckner (son of Johan Nicholas Beckner and Unknown)4 was born Bet. 1733 - 1802 in Baden Germany, died in Franklin County, Virginia. He married Susanna Unknown on Abt. 1756.

 Notes for Lawrence Beckner:
Lawrence is not found on any immigration records, as he would have been seven years old at the time of their arrival in 1740, and children had to be 15 years old to be listed separately from their parents. Lawrence is found on the 1762, 1772, 1779-1783 Tax lists for York County Pensylvania. He is also shown on the 1781 Muster List in York County. The year 1762 appears to be the earliest that the Beckner name is found in this part of Pensylvania. It is assumed that the family resided in Philadelphia (their port city) until about 1760. In 1770, Lawrence's name, as well as the names of Daniel, Nicholas, Johan Nicholas, were found on rolls of the Great Conewago Church.

The first deed found for Lawrence in Virginia is in Botetourt County (Deed BK 3, P. 399) and is dated 8 Aug. 1768. It records the purchase of 200 acres on Looney's Mill Creek for 400 British Pounds Sterling. Lawrence also appears on an 1800 Botetourt Tas List as being over 21 and owning no horses. Lawrence's will, written 21 May 1802 (will Book 8, P23), and probated at the October court that year, gives his land (72 acres ) to his son Daniel and (105 acres) to his son Johnathon. To the remainder of his children, Jacob Beckner, John Beckner, Elizabeth Simmons, and Sussana Garman, he bequethed money (expressed in British pounds). The will was probated October 1802.

Source: Beckners in America, the First 250 years
