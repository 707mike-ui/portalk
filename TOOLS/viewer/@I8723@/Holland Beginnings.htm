This man was a "Hollander" named Dircks. His descendant's names are spelled Duerksen or Dirksen in America.

His father was named C(k)ornelius Early. 18th Century Prussian Records recorded more than one Cornelius Dircks, living near the Montau Mennonite Church. Montau church records show one Cornelius, born about 1715, and another, possibly his son and also named Cornelius (who may have been this man) the father of two similarly recorded men named Johann and David born in the 1760s, their fathers named Cornelius.

Three 1820 Alexanderthal Duerksen settlers were Johann Kornelius Duerksen (#14630 in GRanDMA OnLiine) and his brother David's sons Cornelius David #14634 and Johann David #60181.

Tragheimerweide Church Book, page 34 shows daughter Anna. The next (5 months later) entry is #285089 Cornelius' daughter Catarina.

Johann Dirks sold land at Schweingrube Kr. Stuhm in 1819. From Land Disposals by Mennonites in the Marienwerder District: 1803-1856 Adalbert Goertz.

Tragheimerweide records, page 198: surname is Dierksen. Pages 62, 67, 193 and 273: Diercks. Page 189: Dirck.

Mennonite Migrations shows that his family of 3 males and 2 females emigrated to Alexanderthal in 1819. They were among the first 16 families to settle in 1820. three more families came in 1821.
